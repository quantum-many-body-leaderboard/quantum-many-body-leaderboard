                               Physical solutions of the Kitaev honeycomb model

                                     Fabio  L.  Pedrocchi,1  Stefano  Chesi,1, 2  and  Daniel  Loss1
                 1Department  of  Physics,  University  of  Basel,  Klingelbergstrasse  82,  CH-4056  Basel,  Switzerland
                         2Department  of  Physics,  McGill  University,  Montreal,  Quebec,  Canada  H3A  2T8

                   We  investigate  the  exact  solution  of  the  honeycomb  model  proposed  by  Kitaev  and  derive  an
                explicit formula for the projector onto the physical subspace.  The physical states are simply char-
                acterized by the parity of the total occupation of the fermionic eigenmodes.  We consider a general
                lattice  on  a  torus  and  show  that  the  physical  fermion  parity  depends  in  a  nontrivial  way  on  the
                vortex conﬁguration and the choice of boundary conditions.  In the vortex-free case with a constant
                gauge ﬁeld we are able to obtain an analytical expression of the parity.  For a general conﬁguration
                of the gauge ﬁeld the parity can be easily evaluated numerically, which allows the exact diagonal-
                ization of large spin models.  We consider physically relevant quantities, as in particular the vortex
                energies, and show that their true value and associated states can be substantially diﬀerent from
                the one calculated in the unprojected space, even in the thermodynamic limit.
