                    Motivated by the novel phenomena observed in the layered material SrCu2(BO3)2, the Shastry-Sutherland
                 model (SSM) has been extensively studied as the minimal model for SrCu2(BO3)2.  However, the nature of its
                 quantum phase transition from the plaquette valence-bond solid (PVBS) to antiferromagnetic (AFM) phase is
                 under fierce debate, posing a challenge to understand the underlying quantum criticality. Via the state-of-the-art
                 tensor network simulations, we study the ground state of the SSM on large-scale size up to 20× 20 sites.  We
                 identify the continuous transition nature accompanied by an emergent O(4) symmetry between the PVBS and
                 AFM phase, which strongly suggests a deconfined quantum critical point (DQCP). Furthermore, we map out the
                 phase diagram of an extended SSM that can be continuously tuned to the SSM, which demonstrates the same
                 DQCP phenomena along a whole critical line. Our results indicate a compelling scenario for understanding the
                 origin of the proposed proximate DQCP in recent experiments of SrCu2(BO3)2.
