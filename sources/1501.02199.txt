                  We show how a ground state trial wavefunction of a Fermi liquid can be systematically improved
               introducing a sequence of renormalized coordinates through an iterative backﬂow transformation.
               We apply this scheme to calculate the ground state energy of liquid 3He in two dimensions at freezing
               density  using  variational  and  ﬁxed-node  diﬀusion  Monte  Carlo.   Comparing  with  exact  transient
               estimate results for systems with small number of particles,  we ﬁnd that variance extrapolations
               provide accurate results for the true ground state together with stringent lower bounds.  For larger
               systems these bounds can in turn be used to quantify the systematic bias of ﬁxed-node calculations.
               These wave functions are size consistent and the scaling of their computational complexity with the
               number of particles is the same as for standard backﬂow wave functions.
