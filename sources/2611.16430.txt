Multi-reference variational states resolve stripe selection and local  d-wave pairing in the
                                                             Hubbard model

                                                Honghui Shang1,∗  and Jinlong Yang1,†
                                    1State  Key  Laboratory  of  Precision  and  Intelligent  Chemistry,

                  Whether stripes compete with d-wave superconductivity or promote pairing through fluctuations
                remains unsettled in the two-dimensional Hubbard model. Using multi-reference neural variational
                auxiliary-field quantum Monte Carlo (MR-nVAFQMC) at U/t = 8, we superpose unpaired Slater de-
                terminants across stripe periods and domain-wall positions, reaching variational energies competitive
                with leading benchmarks on periodic lattices up to32   × 32sites. For cuprate next-nearest-neighbor
                hopping (t′/t = −0.2), diagonal hopping preserves electron-doped antiferromagnetism while kinet-
                ically frustrating hole motion into Yamada stripes with a dome of short-range connected d-wave
                pairing. At1 /8hole doping, hole delocalization across antiphase spin boundaries outweighs on-site
                repulsion, splitting the filled stripes of the pure model into half-filled walls with strong local pairing.
                Coherently delocalizing shifted walls strongly suppresses static charge modulation yet leaves short-
                range pairing unchanged, showing that local pairing is intrinsic to half-filled walls rather than driven
                by stripe fluctuations. Across intervening antiferromagnetic domains, however, pairing correlations
                become unresolved at one stripe period and beyond. Both parameter transfer to larger lattices
                without reoptimization and deep benchmark checkpoints retain stripe spin order without resolving
                long-range pairing, within uncertainties allowing weak superconductivity. Half-filled antiphase walls
                thus generate local d-wave pairs, whereas suppressing static stripe order alone yields no resolved
                coherence across stripes in the states studied.




                                                           I.  INTRODUCTION

   How unidirectional spin and charge stripes interact with d-wave superconductivity has been a central question
in the physics of high-Tc cuprates for more than three decades [1–3]. In La2−x    SrxCuO4, neutron scattering reveals
incommensurate magnetic peaks displaced from(       π, π)by  ϵspin, which grows approximately linearly with hole concen-
tration (ϵspin ≈δ  , the Yamada relation) up to δ≈   1/8before saturating at higher doping [ 4]. The suppression of bulk
superconductivity near1       /8doping in   La2−x    BaxCuO4 is the longstanding1                   /8anomaly[        5]. In La1.875  Ba0.125  CuO4,
static period-four charge order (λc  = 4) and period-eight antiphase spin order (  λs  = 8) coexist with a strongly
depressed bulk three-dimensional (3D) transition at Tc ≈ 4 K, whereas transport and magnetization measurements
indicate two-dimensional (2D) superconducting fluctuations below about40    K and a Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless
transition near16    K [6]. A proposed explanation for the suppressed interlayer coherence is an intra-plane pair-density
wave (PDW), whose pairing phase alternates across adjacent stripes and whose stripe direction rotates by90  ◦ between
layers, frustrating interlayer Josephson coupling [2, 7]. Because unidirectional antiphase stripes of charge period λc
and linear hole filling f = δλc carry magnetic incommensurability ϵspin = δ/(2f), the observed slope   ϵspin = δ points
directly to half-filled domain walls (f = 1/2) rather than filled walls (  f = 1,   ϵspin = δ/2). Whether a single-layer 2D
Hubbard model atδ= 1/8develops phase coherence across half-filled stripes—as uniformd-wave or antiphase PDW
order—or supports only short-range intra-wall pairing remains a central microscopic question [2,8–10].
   Whether the single-band 2D repulsive Hubbard model captures these intertwined phenomena has been investigated
intensively with modern many-body methods [11–16]. For the nearest-neighbor model (t′ = 0) at   δ = 1/8and   U/t = 8,
combined density-matrix renormalization group (DMRG), auxiliary-field quantum Monte Carlo (AFQMC), density-
matrix embedding, and tensor-network calculations established an insulating filled-stripe ground state (λc = 8, λs = 16)
without long-range superconductivity [17–19]. Negative next-nearest-neighbor hopping t′/t < 0, relevant to cuprate
Fermi surfaces and electron–hole asymmetry [20–22], favors shorter stripe wavelengths; atδ= 1/8, infinite projected
entangled-pair states find period-four stripes with suppressed superconductivity [23]. Pairing also depends sensitively
on cylinder width: four-leg calculations find algebraically decaying pairing at finite t′ [24], whereas eight-leg calculations
find extremely short-ranged superconducting correlations for t′ ≤ 0[ 25]. In a distinct setting, periodically modulating
nearest-neighbor hopping enhances superconducting correlations on four- and six-leg cylinders [26]. Finite-temperature

calculations show how symmetry projection and multi-determinant improvements change both energies and correlation
functions [39].
   Two methodological considerations motivate probing this competing regime with multi-reference unpaired auxiliary-
field wavefunctions. First, a single-reference variational state—even after space-group projection—inherits a single
reference stripe period and rigid inter-wall spacing, preventing inequivalent stripe periods and shifted domain-wall
arrangements from competing or fluctuating coherently within one quantum state. Second, variational families differ
in how pairing enters their reference states. Canonical number-projected BCS and Pfaffian wavefunctions conserve
total particle number just as Slater determinants do, but embed explicit pairing fields into their reference geminals; by
contrast, each individual unprojected particle-conserving Slater determinant |Φk⟩ carries no explicit pairing field and
has vanishing connected two-body pairing (Cp = 0), whereas space-group projection, multi-determinant superposition,
and the shared imaginary-time auxiliary-field projector generate non-Wick four-fermion correlations without geminal
pairing initialization. Although a variational upper bound on total energy implies neither upper nor lower bounds on
order parameters, reaching competitive energies from particle-conserving Slater pools provides a complementary test of
which features of stripe order govern local pair formation and which limit long-distance coherence.

                                              IV.  DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION

   Using MR-nVAFQMC wavefunctions built from particle-conserving Slater determinants without explicit pairing
fields—at variational energies matching or improving upon leading neural- and tensor-network benchmarks up to
32 × 32sites—our single-layer calculations establish a clear separation between intra-stripe local pair formation and
inter-stripe phase coherence in the 2D t–t′–U Hubbard model. Negative next-nearest-neighbor hopping t′/t = −0.2
switches the δ = 1/8state from filled (  λc = 8) to half-filled (  λc = 4, λs = 8) antiphase stripes obeying the Yamada
relation ϵspin  = δ, stabilized by transverse kinetic delocalization against an opposing Hubbard repulsion penalty.
Crucially, each half-filled domain wall already hosts robust short-range d-wave singlet pairing: the connected intra-wall
vertex is enhanced more than fivefold over the filled stripes at t′ = 0, absent on the electron-doped Néel branch,
peaking on the overdoped side near δ = 3/16where period-eight spin stripe order softens, and unchanged within
uncertainty at δ = 1/8whether the walls are pinned or coherently delocalized across translated stripe sectors so that
static charge modulation drops threefold.
   Comparing these strictly single-layer results with the experimental1 /8anomaly in   La1.875  Ba0.125  CuO4 highlights
both an encouraging microscopic agreement and an instructive open tension [2, 5–7]. Experimentally, bulk 3D super-
conductivity at x = 1/8occurs at only   Tc ≈ 4 K [6], whereas each individual CuO2 layer exhibits 2D superconducting
fluctuations setting in near the spin-stripe ordering scale (∼ 40 K) and reaches a 2D zero-resistance state near16     K;
the suppression of 3D Tc arises from interlayer Josephson decoupling, widely attributed to an intra-plane period-eight
PDW that alternates sign between adjacent stripes and rotates by90  ◦ from layer to layer [2, 6, 7]. On the local
scale, our finding that half-filled λc  = 4domain walls intrinsically form short-range   d-wave pairs—with uniform
q= (0  , 0)and period-eight PDWq= (         π/4, 0)modes nearly degenerate in64-site pair-matrix spectra because inter-wall
Josephson coupling across the antiphase spin domain is very weak—captures the microscopic origin of intra-wall
pairing and near-degenerate0- versus                           π-phase inter-stripe channels. On the macroscopic 2D scale, however, our
single-layer states show transverse pair correlations decaying within one stripe spacing (∆  x = 4) and resolve no
long-range 2D superconducting order up to32   × 32, in contrast to the 2D planar superconductivity observed below
16 K in La1.875  Ba0.125  CuO4 [6] and consistent with the weak or statistically unresolved 2D pairing in lower-energy
striped Pfaffian and NN-fPEPS wavefunctions [10].
   Three concrete possibilities can reconcile this tension and define a clear roadmap for future work. First, a weak
2D intra-plane condensate at the scale corresponding to TBKT ≈ 16 K∼    4 × 10−3  t—such as the small amplitude
∆SC = 0.007(2)(∆              2SC ≈ 5 × 10−5 ) suggested by D = 4NN-fPEPS [ 10]—lies at the statistical noise floor of our present
distant-window estimator, and the spatially uniform average∆          2SC,4b     (L)cancels any sign-alternatingq= (                          π/4, 0)PDW
component. Second, although unified same-checkpoint evaluations on the lowest-energy TableIwavefunctions at
16 × 16and32        × 32confirm strong stripe spin order with unresolved four-bond∆            2SC,4b   , the common-depth multi-size
series in Fig.5B,C still relies on transferred16                    × 16parameters on24      × 24and32     × 32under frame-pinned    D2 ×Z    spin2
symmetry (A4  > 0). Independent per-size variational re-optimization at matched deep   τ, combined with direct
superfluid-stiffness and translation-restored multi-sector scaling [45, 46], will test whether full large-torus relaxation
resolves weak 2D uniform or PDW coherence. Third, if converged ground states of the pure single-band 2D t–t′–U
model at δ = 1/8remain devoid of long-range 2D order, then stabilizing planar coherence at commensurate1                       /8lock-in
requires ingredients beyond the minimal single-band Hubbard Hamiltonian—such as multi-orbital  CuO2 physics,
longer-range interactions, or low-temperature-tetragonal lattice couplings [2, 3, 7]—or doping away from1   /8lock-in
into theδ≳3/16regime where spin stripes soften.
