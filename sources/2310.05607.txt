                           Neural network variational Monte Carlo for positronic chemistry

                                Gino Cassella1*, W.M.C. Foulkes1, David Pfau2,1, and James S. Spencer2
                              1Dept. of Physics, Imperial College London, London SW7 2AZ, United Kingdom and

                 Quantum chemical calculations of the ground-state properties of positron-molecule complexes are challenging.
                 The main difficulty lies in employing an appropriate basis set for representing the coalescence between electrons
                 and a positron. Here, we tackle this problem with the recently developed Fermionic neural network (FermiNet)
                 wavefunction, which does not depend on a basis set. We find that FermiNet produces highly accurate, in some
                 cases state-of-the-art, ground-state energies across a range of atoms and small molecules with a wide variety
                 of qualitatively distinct positron binding characteristics.  We calculate the binding energy of the challenging
                 non-polar benzene molecule, finding good agreement with the experimental value, and obtain annihilation rates
                 which compare favourably with those obtained with explicitly correlated Gaussian wavefunctions.  Our results
                 demonstrate a generic advantage of neural network wavefunction-based methods and broaden their applicability
                 to systems beyond the standard molecular Hamiltonian.
